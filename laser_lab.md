The **L**earning **a**nd **SE**nsing **R**esearch (LaSER) lab conducts fundamental research to develop novel AI and robotics technologies that transform agri-food systems.

In particular, our interdisciplinary resarch aims to: 

- Identify/Address *underexplored* agri-food tasks where AI and robotics technologies can deliver significant benefits, and 
- *Advance* the fields of AI and robotics to enable reliable, robust, and long-term autonomy in real-world environments. 


# Research Projects


### Nighttime Agri-Robotics

  <img width="120" src="../images/agrinight2.gif">

We create AI models that enable robots to reliably perform agricultural tasks (e.g., pest management, harvesting, etc.) at night.

we have released the [**AgriNight**](https://github.com/mamorobel/AgriNight) dataset, the first day-and-night image dataset with semantic annotations for visual navigation. 
we have also developed a novel multimodal day-to-night image translation method that enables the reuse of datyimte annotations to train nighttime segmentation models for autonomous visual navigation in agricultural fields.  


### Anomaly Detection in Crop Images

<!-- <div style="min-height: 170px;"> -->
<img style="margin: 0 0 0 20px; float: right;" width="200" src="../images/anomaly_detection.gif">

We develop AI-based anomaly detection methods for crop monitoring robots to identify unhealthy crops and other abnormalities that are rarely represented in training datasets. 
Such automated detection aims to enable early  intervention, either autonomously or through human involvement, improving farm management and agricultural productivity.  

We have introduced the [**Riseholme-2021**](https://github.com/ctyeong/Riseholme-2021) dataset and [**Channel Randomisation**](https://github.com/ctyeong/CH-Rand), a novel self-supervised representation learning method that learns an embedding space in which anomalies can be effectively distinguished, even when no anomalous samples are available during training.

  
### Intelligent Olfactory Sensing

<img style="margin: 0 0 0 20px; float: right;" width="280" src="../images/e_nose_research.gif">

We design AI models to enhance the capabilities of machine olfactory sensing using electronic noses (E-Noses), with broad potential applications in agri-food systems. 
Our research focuses on effective representation learning, sensor drift mitigation, and generalization to complex, unseen environments.

We conducted [pioneering research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27672) investigating the potential and limitations of time-series foundation models for learning from E-Nose data.
